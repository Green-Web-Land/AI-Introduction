# AI Introduction — Young Children

**Explore ideas. Ask questions. Keep your own choices.**

An adult-led course for approximately **ages 6–8**, using short stories, pictures and activities on paper. The age range is an editorial target, not tested suitability or permission to use an online service.

**Start with a parent, carer, educator or another trusted adult.** No child account, live AI conversation, WebAI installation or purchase is required. All activity materials are supplied in the lessons. The adult can read aloud; the child can speak, point, draw, use an accessible equivalent or take a break.

## What is included

Ten complete lessons, five original picture aids, a curriculum, simple glossary and adult/developer guidance. Every lesson has one idea, a read-aloud explanation, a fictional story, an activity, adult answer guidance and another way to try it.

All story characters and answer cards are invented. No example is a captured AI response. No child needs to browse, upload a picture, give personal information, encounter upsetting content or prove that they can use a tool alone.

The course introduces useful distinctions: people and tools, ideas and instructions, patterns and certainty, answers and evidence, private and fictional information. “Imagine tomorrow” explores possible futures rather than predicting what will happen in five years.

## Before reading with a child

Read the [parent/educator guide](PARENT-EDUCATOR-GUIDE.md) first. Adapt pace and language to the child. Keep adult notes and source pages for adults; some references discuss online harms in detail. Do not open those pages as a child activity.

Adults retain responsibility for suitable material and help. Completing this course is not a safety certificate, permission for unsupervised AI use or proof of learning effectiveness. No learner pilot, independent child-development review or formal accessibility assessment has been performed.
