**Course introduction · full course planned.**

An adult-led introduction for approximately ages 6–8, using stories, pictures and offline activities.

## Planned coverage

Distinguish a person from a tool; practise asking a trusted adult; recognise when to stop.

## Example of a planned activity

Choose which fictional requests need help from a trusted adult.

This is an outline of a future activity, not a completed lesson or a tested learning outcome.

## Audience and boundaries

The age range is an editorial target, not tested suitability or permission to use a service. No child account or live AI conversation will be required.

Core activities will use fictional information and offer an offline route. No WebAI installation or AI account will be required. Full lessons will state their audience, practical requirements, sources and guidance for supporters.
