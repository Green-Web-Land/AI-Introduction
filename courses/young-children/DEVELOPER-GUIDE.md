# Developer reuse guide

Copy the approved course folder from a pinned revision, with its licence, source notes and asset provenance. Preserve its relative paths and adult-led framing.

## Package

Ten lesson Markdown files, eight supporting Markdown files, and five SVG/PNG image pairs. No executable code, data collection or live AI connection is supplied in the course.

Keep child-facing sections and adult guidance distinguishable. Preserve read-aloud use, alt text, adjacent picture descriptions, skip options and fictional labels. Sources are for adults; do not turn them into a child browsing exercise.

Do not:

- convert the paper examples into live prompts or automatic submissions;
- request child accounts, names, addresses, images or personal stories;
- animate the screen as a sentient caregiver or emotionally dependent companion;
- turn discussion observations into a safety rating or eligibility test;
- remove the limits beside the pattern game or future stories.

## Integration checks

Resolve local links, render PNGs without cropping, and test text alternatives, keyboard access and assistive technology in the actual host. These files do not establish product-runtime readiness or formal accessibility conformance.

If adding accounts, analytics, forms, speech recording, generated responses or storage, assess the actual feature and child-data practices separately before use. Do not infer that the host collects no data merely because this folder is static. No product age permission is granted by the editorial age range.

Translations and age adaptations need review for meaning, audience and local context. Retain [CC BY 4.0 notices](LICENSE.md), credit and source links, and describe changes. Do not imply author or source-organisation endorsement.
