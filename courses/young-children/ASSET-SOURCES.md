# Original picture aids

All five SVG drawings were code-authored for this course with AI assistance; PNGs are rendered from them. No third-party pictures, logos, real children's photographs or product screenshots are included.

| File stem under images/ | Used in | Picture description |
|---|---|---|
| 01-people-tools | Home, Lesson 1 | An adult and child, pencil and faceless computer screen distinguish people from tools. |
| 02-patterns | Lesson 3 | Two shape rows with a question at the end; the stated repeat determines the intended continuation. |
| 03-check | Lesson 5 | Three stars beside a fictional answer of four; count together. |
| 04-help | Lesson 8 | A blank screen, pause symbol and adult/child figures with a help phrase. |
| 05-tomorrow | Lesson 10 | Two possible story-making scenes, with and without a suggestion card; neither is a forecast. |

Every PNG has a corresponding SVG with a title and description. Lessons include alt text and adjacent picture words. Meaning is not carried by colour alone. People are stylised figures, not representations of actual participants or an instruction about family structure.

The AI screen has no eyes, smile, body or emotional expression. The scenes are invented and do not portray a real AI application. Images are instructional aids, not evidence of tested learning outcomes.
