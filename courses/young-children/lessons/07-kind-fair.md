# 7. Kind and fair choices

[Course home](../README.md) · [Adult guide](../PARENT-EDUCATOR-GUIDE.md)

**For a grown-up to read with a child.** All stories and answer cards are invented. No AI tool or account is used.

## Our idea

Choose a respectful action instead of obeying an unfair suggestion.

## Read together

A tool's idea is not always a kind or fair idea. People need to think about others.

We can make something that includes people. We ask before using someone else's picture or sharing their work.

## Little story

The pretend story group has three characters who all want to help. An invented suggestion says, “Only the character with a red hat can draw.”

The children choose a different plan: everyone may choose a way to help. One draws, one tells a story and one points to ideas.

## Try together

What is wrong with the red-hat rule? Choose a new plan: “Only one hat colour may help” or “Each character can choose a way to join.”

Then imagine that a character does not want their drawing shared. Choose: keep it with them or share it anyway. Use fictional work only.

## For the grown-up

Hat colour does not determine who can contribute. The inclusive plan is appropriate to this story; keeping the drawing respects the stated choice. Do not sort real children by ability, identity or belongings. Permission is not the only possible safeguard, and this activity grants no right to publish a real child's material. Source S3 informs the fairness principle. [Source notes for adults](../SOURCES.md).

## Try another way

Change the story so someone would rather watch or pause. Let that be a choice too. Participation is not compulsory.
