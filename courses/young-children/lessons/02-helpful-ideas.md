# 2. Ideas a tool can offer

[Course home](../README.md) · [Adult guide](../PARENT-EDUCATOR-GUIDE.md)

**For a grown-up to read with a child.** All stories and answer cards are invented. No AI tool or account is used.

## Our idea

Recognise an idea as something people can choose to use.

## Read together

Some AI tools can suggest words or make pictures. Different tools do different jobs. A suggestion is an idea, not an order.

You can also get ideas by talking, playing, reading or drawing. You do not need an AI tool to make something.

## Little story

Leo wants a title for an imaginary picture of a moon garden. The adult shows two invented suggestion cards: “Moon Flowers” and “A Garden in Space.”

Leo says, “I want to call it My Quiet Moon.” Leo has made a new choice. No computer is being used.

## Try together

The adult reads these cards:

- A: Suggest a title for our pretend picture.
- B: Replace the grown-up who cares for a child.
- C: Promise that every answer is right.

Which describes something some AI tools can help with? Make up your own title too. You can speak, point or draw.

## For the grown-up

A is a possible tool task; B and C are not promises to make about AI. Accept any imaginative title. The point is choice, not whether a title is clever. Do not stage a chatbot demonstration. Source S1 supports examples of generated content. [Source notes for adults](../SOURCES.md).

## Try another way

Choose a title without using either suggestion. Or keep one and explain what you like. Both choices are allowed.
