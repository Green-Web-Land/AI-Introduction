# 9. Make it your own

[Course home](../README.md) · [Adult guide](../PARENT-EDUCATOR-GUIDE.md)

**For a grown-up to read with a child.** All stories and answer cards are invented. No AI tool or account is used.

## Our idea

Make a choice and describe where an idea came from.

## Read together

An idea can be a starting point. You can change it, combine it with your own idea or choose not to use it.

When someone or a tool helps, we can say so. That tells others how we made our work.

## Little story

The adult offers an invented suggestion: “Draw a boat with a square sail.” Kai draws a boat with a round window and keeps the square sail.

Kai says, “The card gave me the sail idea. I chose the window.” No AI tool made Kai's picture.

## Try together

Choose a card: square sail, striped kite or moon garden. Draw, describe or point out one thing to keep and one thing you would change. Say or let the adult write: “The card suggested ___. I chose ___.”

## For the grown-up

Any safe imaginative change is welcome. Honest acknowledgement should match what happened: this activity used an authored card, not a live AI response. Do not tell children their drawing is less valuable because they used help. This is a learning practice, not a complete copyright or school-assessment rule. [Source notes for adults](../SOURCES.md).

## Try another way

Invent a completely different picture without a card. A child may prefer this route. There is no requirement to use AI or to make something for public sharing.
