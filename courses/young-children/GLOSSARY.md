# Words to explore together

For a grown-up to explain in context. Children do not need to memorise these words.

| Word | A simple explanation |
|---|---|
| Tool | Something we use to help with a task. |
| AI | Short for artificial intelligence: computer methods used for tasks such as finding patterns or making suggestions. |
| Idea | Something we might try or think about. |
| Pattern | A repeated or related way things fit together. |
| Guess | An answer that may need checking. |
| Check | Look for a good reason to believe an answer. |
| Evidence | Something that helps us check a claim; our three-star card is evidence for its own count. |
| Private | Something about a person that we should not put into this course's activities or a tool. |
| Permission | Someone agrees to something; do not assume their agreement. |
| Trusted grown-up | An adult the child can turn to for care and help; not every familiar adult is safe. |
| Fictional | Made up for a story or activity. |
| Future | A time that has not happened yet. |
| Possible | Something that might happen, not a promise. |

AI is a broad subject. The short explanations support these activities; they are not full technical definitions. An AI “guess” can involve complex computation, not random guessing. [Adult source notes](SOURCES.md)
