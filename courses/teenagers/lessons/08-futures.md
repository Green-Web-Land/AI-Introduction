# Explore possible futures

[Home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Objective:** Build a conditional scenario and choose an action you can evaluate now.

No tool can guarantee what your education, work or relationships will look like in five years. A scenario is a possible future built on assumptions, not a promise.

Imagine an interest in community design. In one year you might learn to explain a proposal clearly. In three years you might join a project that uses AI to compare ideas. In five years you might use different tools or pursue another interest. These are invented possibilities, not evidence-based forecasts.

Access, time, support, health, opportunities and preferences can change. AI use alone does not determine outcomes or personal worth.

## Practise

Create two possible paths:
- A path where suitable AI tools are available.
- A path where you rely on people, ordinary tools and other resources.

For each, identify a useful skill, an assumption, a risk and an action this month. Include a way to change your plan.

Worked example: skill—explaining evidence; action—write a fictional one-page proposal; check—can a reader identify which claims have support? Risk—accepting a made-up source; response—verify the source before using it. This skill is useful on either path.

## Check yourself

Rewrite “AI will guarantee my career” as a testable action. Example: “This month I will practise comparing two supplied sources and explain their differences.” That is something you can attempt and review.

Do not put private future plans or identifying details into an online tool for this activity. You can keep the map offline.

**Transfer task:** name an observation that would make you revise your scenario.

**Takeaway:** prepare through transferable skills and flexible choices. Uncertainty is a reason to revisit a plan, not to invent certainty.

All situations, outputs and worked examples in this lesson are fictional teaching material. No live AI account or submission is required.
