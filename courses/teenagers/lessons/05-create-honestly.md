# Create honestly and respect others

[Home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Objective:** Use assistance transparently while retaining your own choices.

AI can suggest words, pictures or structures. You still need to decide whether the result fits, whether facts are supported, and whether you have permission for the material you use. Access to an image does not by itself grant reuse rights.

Fictional brief: create a short poster for an invented book exchange. A tool offers a slogan, a fake testimonial from a named real author and a copied-looking logo. The slogan may be a starting point; an invented endorsement should not be presented as real, and rights in a logo cannot be assumed.

## Practise

Make a safe version using your own simple design, fictional details and no endorsement claim. Write an assistance note.

Possible note: “I used AI suggestions for initial wording, then selected and revised the text. The event details are fictional.” This describes help; it does not establish ownership of all output.

## Check yourself

Can you submit an AI-written assignment as entirely your own? Follow the teacher's or organiser's rules and be honest about assistance. This general course does not decide what a particular school permits.

For an original creative choice, there may be several good answers. For a factual statement, find evidence. Keep these two kinds of evaluation separate.

**Transfer task:** compare two fictional slogans, choose one and explain your reason without asking a tool to choose for you.

**Takeaway:** use AI as assistance you can explain. Do not fabricate evidence, impersonate someone or imply permission or endorsement that you do not have.

All situations, outputs and worked examples in this lesson are fictional teaching material. No live AI account or submission is required.
