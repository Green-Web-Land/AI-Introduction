# Teenagers curriculum

[Home](README.md)

Read in order, or revisit a topic as needed. Each lesson has an offline exercise and a check. Plan roughly 15–25 minutes per lesson and additional time for the final project; these are untested editorial estimates.

| Lesson | You will practise |
|---|---|
| [Understand AI and choose a task](lessons/01-understand-ai.md) | Explain how an AI model differs from the application and tools around it. |
| [Ask clearly and refine](lessons/02-ask-and-refine.md) | Turn a vague request into a bounded task, then check the result. |
| [Check claims and media](lessons/03-check-media.md) | Separate a circulating claim from evidence that supports it. |
| [Protect privacy and consent](lessons/04-privacy-consent.md) | Minimise information and distinguish access from permission. |
| [Create honestly and respect others](lessons/05-create-honestly.md) | Use assistance transparently while retaining your own choices. |
| [Control actions and recognise unsafe requests](lessons/06-tools-and-boundaries.md) | Identify when to verify, seek permission or stop. |
| [Learn and keep human relationships](lessons/07-learn-and-relate.md) | Use assistance without handing over your learning or judgment about people. |
| [Explore possible futures](lessons/08-futures.md) | Build a conditional scenario and choose an action you can evaluate now. |
| [Apply principles in a final project](lessons/09-principles-and-project.md) | Design a useful bounded AI task and explain its checks and responsibilities. |

## What completion means

Aim to explain a model/application distinction, write a bounded request, check a claim, minimise information, identify permissions, disclose help honestly and apply these skills to a new fictional project. Suggested answers support discussion; they are not a grading authority.

The final project checks whether you can explain your choices and corrections. Completion is not a credential or a measured guarantee of competence. You may pause, ask a trusted person for help, or choose a non-AI method.
