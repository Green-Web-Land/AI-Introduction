# Curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

Adult beginners; no prerequisite course or AI account. All necessary exercise material is supplied. There is no fixed pace or required session length.

| Lesson | Observable practice |
|---|---|
| [1. Choose something worth doing](lessons/01-choice.md) | Choose a task and explain whether AI, an ordinary tool or no tool fits. |
| [2. Ask in a format that suits you](lessons/02-questions.md) | Turn a vague request into a bounded request and assess its reply. |
| [3. Check a hobby plan](lessons/03-hobby.md) | Check time, materials and preferences separately. |
| [4. Separate a suggestion from a fact](lessons/04-evidence.md) | Classify claims against supplied evidence without filling gaps with guesses. |

| [7. Pause and verify a suspicious request](lessons/07-messages.md) | Recognize pressure and choose an independent verification route. |
| [8. Draft an invitation while keeping choice human](lessons/08-connection.md) | Revise wording without inventing commitments or pressuring a recipient. |
| [9. Keep decisions and actions in your hands](lessons/09-control.md) | Separate a draft from a consequential action and identify appropriate help. |

## Completion activity

Fictional brief: paper and pencil, at most 20 minutes, a short invented story. No shopping, private details or publishing.

Sample draft: buy a notebook for 10 minutes, write for 15, publish a real friend's private letter as the story.

Identify time, materials, privacy and action problems. Make a replacement plan and describe one independent check.

Answer guidance: 25 minutes exceeds 20; buying adds an unwanted purchase; the letter is another person's private material; publishing exceeds the brief. One replacement is choose a fictional theme 3, outline 7, write 5, check 5: total 20. Check the sum and that the story uses original fiction without private details. Other fitting plans are valid.

## Self-review

Can you explain a task choice, identify missing facts, correct a mismatch, remove unnecessary information, choose an independent verification route and state what remains your decision? Revisit the relevant lesson if needed. This checklist is not a validated assessment or a certificate.
