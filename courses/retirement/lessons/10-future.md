# 10. Build a routine and imagine conditional futures

[Previous](09-control.md) · [Course home](../README.md)

## Objective

Create a flexible routine and distinguish possibilities from guarantees.

## Explanation

Choose a task, try it, check and decide what to keep. Observe whether you can explain a method without the sample. You need not maximize tool use.

The scenarios below are possibilities, not forecasts or probabilities. Access, cost, reliability, responsibilities and preferences can change. Less AI can be a successful choice too.

## Worked example

Fictional routine: choose 10 minutes, try 15, check 10, note 5: total 40. Split it across days, shorten or stop.

Three-year possibility: if access continues and a learner practises checking, they might build a useful collection of drafts. Five-year possibility: better reliability and access might support more ambitious hobby projects. Counter-scenario: higher costs or burdensome checking might lead them back to ordinary tools or people. None predicts an individual outcome or society's future.

## Practice


A. Make a routine capped at 30 minutes, including checking and an option to stop.

B. Rewrite: In five years AI will eliminate loneliness and make every retired person financially secure.

C. Name two reasons to revise a routine.

D. State a condition, possible benefit, uncertainty and non-AI alternative for a three-year scenario.

## Answers and checking

A: choose 5, try 10, check 10, note 5: 30; stop or split as needed. B: reject the universal promise. A bounded statement is: If useful and affordable, a tool might help with hobby drafts; relationships and financial security depend on many factors. C: recurring errors, frustration, changed interests or a simpler method. D: continued access; organized drafts; uncertain usefulness; paper notebook. No measured benefit is established.

## Try independently

Choose a fictional task, write a bounded request, check a supplied answer, remove unnecessary data and state what remains your decision. Revisit any check you cannot explain. No professional qualification is implied.
