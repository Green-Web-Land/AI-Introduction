# 5. Learn, check and try independently

[Previous](04-evidence.md) · [Course home](../README.md) · [Next](06-privacy.md)

## Objective

Explain a supplied rule and apply it to a new example without copying.

## Explanation

Repeating an answer differs from using an idea. Inspect an explanation, try a new case, then compare. If stuck, ask for a hint about the difficult step rather than replacing the whole attempt.

This is a supplied counting exercise, not a claim about how everyone learns. Use letters instead of coloured tokens, choose your pace and ask a supporter to read without giving the answer.

## Worked example

Rule: one complete unit is two blue tokens followed by one gold token: BBG. Four units contain 8 blue, 4 gold, total 12.

Sample AI answer: Five units contain 10 blue and 10 gold. Check the rule: one gold per unit means 5 gold. Correct total is 15. Clear wording did not make the sample correct.

## Practice


A. Write three units and count each colour.

B. A strip contains 12 blue and 6 gold in this pattern. How many complete units?

C. Explain why the gold count matters.

D. Cover the example and work out seven units.

## Answers and checking

A: BBG BBG BBG: 6 blue, 3 gold, total 9. B: six units; 12/2 and 6/1 both give six. C: one gold in each unit makes gold count equal unit count. D: 14 blue, 7 gold, total 21. If two counting methods disagree, inspect one unit and regroup.

## Try independently

New rule: one square followed by three circles. Two complete units contain 2 squares and 6 circles, total 8. Explain the method before looking at those totals.
