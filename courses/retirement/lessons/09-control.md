# 9. Keep decisions and actions in your hands

[Previous](08-connection.md) · [Course home](../README.md) · [Next](10-future.md)

## Objective

Separate a draft from a consequential action and identify appropriate help.

## Explanation

A sentence saying an action happened is not proof. Some products generate text only; others have connected tools. Before real actions, understand access, the intended change and how to confirm the result independently.

For consequential financial, medical or legal matters, use an appropriately qualified professional or relevant official service. AI may organize general questions but should not be treated as a personal decision-maker. This course gives no investment selection, diagnosis, dosage, entitlement or legal advice.

## Worked example

Request: Draft questions about a workshop.

Reply: I booked it and paid a deposit.

That exceeds drafting. In this offline exercise no tools or booking exist. In real use, check the actual service through its official channel before assuming success or failure. Do not repeat a payment blindly.

## Practice

Classify as a draft or consequential decision/action:

A. Fictional questions about workshop times.

B. Pick a real investment for a person.

C. General questions for an adviser, with no account data.

D. Send a booking payment.

E. Change prescribed treatment based on an answer.

For B, D and E, name a better next step without performing it.

## Answers and checking

A and C are drafts requiring review. B: discuss the personal decision with an appropriately qualified adviser. D: independently establish legitimacy and terms, then make your own decision before any authorized payment. E: contact the treating professional rather than act on the generated change. No exercise requires a transaction or treatment change.

## Try independently

State what a fictional tool may draft, what it must not do, who decides and how an actual outcome would be verified. A prompt alone is not a technical permission control.
