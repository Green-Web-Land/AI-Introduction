# How to Use AI at Retirement

[AI Introduction home](../../README.md)

Learn to use AI well—and remain able to think, choose and act for yourself.

A complete beginner course for adults exploring interests, learning and everyday projects. Retirement is a life context, not an age threshold or ability rating. No assumptions are made about income, health, household or technical experience. This adult-focused course is not designed as an unsupervised course for children.

## Start here

Use the lessons in order or revisit a topic. Each includes an explanation, fictional example, supplied practice, reasoned answers and a new task. Try the practice before reading the answers. Work on paper, type or ask a supporter to read aloud. Choose your pace and breaks. No AI account, purchase, installation or real personal information is needed.

## What you can practise

Choose a task, write a bounded request, check a plan, separate evidence from guesses, learn independently, protect private information and retain control. These are learning goals, not demonstrated learner outcomes.

## Boundaries

General education, not personal financial, medical or legal advice, a safety guarantee or a professional qualification. No real messages, payments or bookings are part of the course. Future scenarios are conditional possibilities. An ordinary tool, a trusted person or no tool can be appropriate.

AI-assisted text and diagrams were editorially checked. No learner trial or formal accessibility evaluation has been conducted. Source dates and limitations are recorded in the source notes.
