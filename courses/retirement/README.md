Exploring useful assistance for interests, learning and everyday projects at a comfortable pace.

## Planned coverage

Ask clear questions; verify messages; recognise pressure or scams; choose accessible ways to learn.

## Example of a planned activity

Check a fictional hobby plan and identify which facts need a reliable source.

This is an outline of a future activity, not a completed lesson or a tested learning outcome.

## Audience and boundaries

No previous AI experience will be assumed. Retirement is a life context, not an ability rating. AI does not replace personal financial or medical guidance.

Core activities will use fictional information and offer an offline route. No WebAI installation or AI account will be required. Full lessons will state their audience, practical requirements, sources and guidance for supporters.
