## Original teaching material

The question exercises, search log, fictional catalogue, policy cards, records A–D, two dataset versions, pseudocode, portfolio and future scenarios were authored for this course with AI assistance and editorial checking. They are not real studies, actual student records, product transcripts or external research instruments.

The analysis uses elementary arithmetic and explicitly limited interpretation. The future scenarios carry no probabilities or guarantees. No classroom trial, formal accessibility evaluation, accredited assessment or comprehensive legal review is claimed. Sources do not validate the particular teaching sequence.

External pages may change. The unavailable older UTM service URL was replaced with the current University of Toronto Libraries listing above; the inaccessible page was not used for substantive claims.
