# Curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

## Audience and preparation

Postsecondary learners, primarily adult beginners. Some students are minors; local rules and support needs still apply. No live AI or research participation is required. Bring paper or a local editor and basic arithmetic familiarity. Use the supplied record packet in Lesson 4 for subsequent evidence exercises.

## Sequence and intended outcomes

| Lesson | Outcome |
|---|---|
| [1. Define the question before asking AI](lessons/01-question.md) | Turn a broad topic into a question with a clear evidence need. |
| [2. Separate permission from verification](lessons/02-rules.md) | Apply a stated rule without assuming it covers a different task. |
| [3. Build a search plan](lessons/03-search.md) | Use search suggestions as leads while recording what you actually searched. |
| [4. Trace a citation to a record](lessons/04-citations.md) | Check source identity separately from the claim it is said to support. |
| [5. Compare methods before conclusions](lessons/05-evidence.md) | Explain why differing records may not answer the same question. |
| [6. Write a synthesis you can defend](lessons/06-synthesis.md) | Connect evidence and limitations in your own argument. |
| [7. Check a small analysis](lessons/07-data.md) | Calculate the stated summaries and keep their interpretation bounded. |
| [8. Leave a method another reader can inspect](lessons/08-method.md) | Trace a rule and document a reproducible calculation on paper. |
| [9. Protect material and describe contributions](lessons/09-responsibility.md) | Choose permitted inputs and give an accurate account of assistance. |
| [10. Build a workflow and examine future possibilities](lessons/10-workflow.md) | Choose a process you can explain and revise as evidence changes. |

Each lesson contains a worked example, supplied practice, answer reasoning and a transfer task. Suggested timing is 25–40 minutes per lesson, adjustable and untested. Learning can be spread across sessions. Keep the two versions of Record A separate; the correction is intentionally introduced for practice.

## Evidence of understanding

The final portfolio includes a bounded question, permission decision, source check, comparison, calculation/method record and accurate account of assistance. A learner should explain why a plausible claim is unsupported, not simply repeat a warning about AI.

Use [feedback criteria](EDUCATOR-GUIDE.md) to identify what needs revision. These are educational prompts, not a validated assessment, credential or prediction of academic performance. Reading the course does not authorize any real research activity, data upload or assessed use.
