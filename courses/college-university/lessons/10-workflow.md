# 10. Build a workflow and examine future possibilities

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Choose a process you can explain and revise as evidence changes.

## Explanation

Our suggested workflow is: define the question; check permission; inspect sources; perform your own analysis; check the result; explain assistance and limits. You may choose no AI. This is a teaching method, not a validated intervention or promise of better grades.

Keep a short record of your question, sources inspected, versions, calculations, corrections and unresolved questions. Do not equate speed or polished prose with understanding.

## Worked example

A fictional learner uses permitted search-term suggestions, inspects records A and C, calculates version 1 and version 2 separately, and writes a limited conclusion. The work record shows exactly what changed. A learner who simply copies an unsupported summary has not shown those checks, even if the summary looks polished.

## Prediction: three- and five-year scenarios

In three years, regular practice with checking and independent explanation might leave you with a useful portfolio of reasoning. Collecting unexamined outputs might leave gaps hidden. Neither result is inevitable.

In five years, better teaching, equitable access and suitable safeguards could support useful assistance across disciplines. Poor implementation or unequal access could limit that benefit. These are conditional possibilities, not forecasts with probabilities. No degree, job, income or learning gain is promised.

## Practice

Create a six-part portfolio: a bounded question; a permission decision; a citation check; a comparison of A and C; a versioned calculation/method note; and an honest activity statement. Include one unresolved question and a reasonable next step. No public submission or personal story is required.

## Answer and reasoning

Attempt the practice before reading this section.

Use the [educator guide](../EDUCATOR-GUIDE.md) to check whether your claims match the records, calculations retain correct versions and units, and the note accurately describes assistance. Missing information should remain missing. Revise what you cannot yet explain. The portfolio is practice, not a professional credential.

## Transfer task

Write “If …, then … might …; this depends on …; I would check …” about your learning in three years. Include a skill you will practise without AI and evidence that would make you change the plan. A reasonable answer names assumptions and observable work rather than promising success. Discuss disciplinary differences with an instructor when applying this workflow beyond the supplied examples.
