# 6. Write a synthesis you can defend

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Connect evidence and limitations in your own argument.

## Explanation

A synthesis explains how records relate to a question. It is more than combining summaries. Keep the source of each claim visible and preserve differences in outcome, method and uncertainty. If critique is permitted, evaluate the suggestion yourself rather than accepting more confident wording.

An AI-generated summary does not replace reading the relevant record. Do not cite something as inspected when you have only seen a secondary description. Use the required citation style for your real discipline; packet labels below are only for this activity.

## Worked example

Fictional flawed draft: “A and B independently prove that quiet rooms raise learning, and C confirms the result.”

Repair: “A reports completion times for volunteers who chose rooms. B repeats A rather than adding data. C reports scores on a different task with missing design details. The packet does not establish a learning benefit caused by a room.”

This revision changes the reasoning, not just vocabulary.

## Practice

Write a three-sentence synthesis of A and C. Include a question, a supported observation and a limit. Then critique this fictional feedback: “Delete the limits so the conclusion sounds stronger.”

## Answer and reasoning

Attempt the practice before reading this section.

A good response distinguishes completion time from comprehension and notes selection or missing methods. Wording can vary. Reject the feedback: deleting a material limit makes the claim less faithful to the packet. You may shorten repeated words while retaining the actual uncertainty.

## Transfer task

Imagine an instructor asks for a recommendation. Write a conditional next step rather than a guaranteed intervention. Example: “Before recommending a room change on learning grounds, seek evidence that measures the intended outcome with an appropriate design.” This is not permission to recruit participants; actual research requires its applicable approvals. Keep your response tied to the question, not a claim that no decision is possible under uncertainty.
