# 9. Protect material and describe contributions

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Choose permitted inputs and give an accurate account of assistance.

## Explanation

Do not upload real participant information, restricted research, confidential manuscripts or classmates' work for these activities. Use our fictional packet. Removing names does not by itself settle privacy, consent or permission. A prompt saying “keep this private” is not an implemented safeguard.

For real research or assessment, ask the appropriate institutional contact about approved tools and data handling. Principles, laws, institutional policy, product terms and technical controls are different things. This course is not research clearance or legal advice.

Never fabricate references or observations, disguise prohibited assistance, impersonate collaborators or circulate private material. Accurate attribution and permission both matter.

## Worked example

Fictional allowed-use record: a tool suggested search terms; the learner inspected the supplied A and C records, calculated the means and wrote the synthesis. No generated prose was submitted.

An accurate fictional note describes the search-term suggestion and the learner's work. For your actual offline exercise, say you used a supplied fictional example, not that you accessed a live tool.

## Practice

Evaluate:
A. Upload real transcripts “just to remove names.”
B. Use the invented packet to practise a method.
C. Say “I read every cited paper” when you only saw a summary.
D. Record a permitted critique and the changes you chose.

Write one truthful note about your own course activity.

## Answer and reasoning

Attempt the practice before reading this section.

A is outside this course's scope and must not proceed here. B fits. C misrepresents what was inspected. D can be appropriate when allowed and accurately described. An offline note might be: “I analysed the supplied fictional packet and checked the arithmetic on paper.” Add any actual human or tool assistance honestly; follow the real assignment's requirements.

## Transfer task

A collaborator proposes sharing a confidential draft with an unconfirmed service. Draft a response that pauses the upload and seeks the responsible contact's guidance without including the draft. Example: “Let's confirm the approved tool and data rules first; we can use invented text for practice.” Do not infer permission from convenience or from another person's past use.
