# Developer and adaptation guide

[Course home](README.md)

## Content package

Ten Markdown lessons, eight supporting documents and five SVG/PNG pairs. No executable application, data-collection form, tracking script, API call or learner account is included. Host practices are separate from the files.

Keep lesson identifiers and relative links stable. SVGs are editable originals; PNGs provide GitHub display. Retain descriptive alt text and the [text equivalents](ASSET-SOURCES.md); do not rely on colour alone. Check reading order, contrast, zoom and assistive-technology behaviour in your own interface. This package has no formal accessibility certification.

## Preserve the meaning

Retain the fictional labels on every policy, source packet and dataset. Record A's corrected version is a separate input, not a silent replacement. Zero and missing values have different roles. Do not turn the paper examples into claims about real studies or benchmarked models.

Keep objectives, examples, practice, answers and transfer tasks. An optional answer-reveal interface should still permit offline completion; that is design guidance, not a new licence restriction. Do not imply that a completed portfolio is a degree, research permission or misconduct clearance.

## Integration and rights

[CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md) permits commercial adaptation subject to its terms and the rights held. Credit the source, link the licence, retain required notices and mark changes. External reference pages retain their own terms; they are not bundled course assets. In particular, linking ACRL's framework does not relicense its text under this course's licence.

For reproducible integration, pin the chosen content revision and preserve its asset files. Recheck sources and local rules when adapting. A live AI service, stored submissions, analytics or participant-data workflow requires its own technical, institutional and legal assessment. Content reuse permission does not grant rights to third-party data or confer platform eligibility.
