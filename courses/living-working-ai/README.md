# Living and Working with AI

**Your choices matter. Learn. Question. Decide.**

Understand a changing world, build useful skills and take part in decisions about AI. This eight-lesson course connects everyday tasks with changes in work and society. You do not need technical knowledge or an AI account.

Start with a familiar situation. Practise using assistance, examine whose experience matters, and finish with a learning plan you can explain and revise.

For adult beginners. Read alone, with a willing supporter or in a group. Use paper, spoken answers or a local document. All practice records and stories are invented. Each lesson supplies what you need; online source reading and live AI use are optional.

Allow roughly 25–40 minutes per lesson, with breaks and extra practice when useful. These are editorial planning estimates, not tested completion times. The 30-day plan is a flexible follow-on activity, not a deadline for completing the course.

## A habit to carry with you

**Learn.** What is happening, and what could help me understand it?

**Question.** What supports this answer? What is missing? Who could be affected?

**Decide.** What will I do, and why? What would make me reconsider?

You can ask for help, use AI for part of a task, choose another method or pause. Your choices matter alongside those of employers, schools, public institutions and technology providers. Access, opportunity and support matter too.
