# Course map

[Course home](README.md)

**Your choices matter. Learn. Question. Decide.**

Eight complete lessons take you from a small everyday choice to decisions about work, society and your own learning. No additional lesson is required to complete this edition.

| Route | Central question | Work you can demonstrate |
|---|---|---|
| [1. Everyday life](lessons/01-everyday-life.md) | Where could AI help, and what needs checking? | Correct a message and explain a choice |
| [2. Working with AI](lessons/02-working-with-ai.md) | How can I explain a task and improve the result? | Write a brief and adapt an agenda to changed conditions |
| [3. Evidence and trust](lessons/03-evidence-and-trust.md) | What deserves my trust? | Trace a claim and update it when new evidence arrives |
| [4. Changing work](lessons/04-changing-work.md) | Which tasks could change, and who shapes that change? | Map work, compare a whole task and propose a shared improvement |
| [5. AI in society](lessons/05-ai-in-society.md) | Who benefits, who faces barriers and who can influence a decision? | Examine participation data and explain a public-service trade-off |
| [6. Skills worth developing](lessons/06-skills-worth-developing.md) | How can I strengthen knowledge, judgment and collaboration? | Explain a method and use it on a changed example |
| [7. Responsibility and choice](lessons/07-responsibility-and-choice.md) | What information, review and human involvement fit the situation? | Set boundaries and question a selection rule |
| [8. Your next 30 days](lessons/08-next-30-days.md) | What small action is worth taking now? | Connect the lessons in a final case and build a learning plan |

## Three connected perspectives

**My task:** understand, create and check. Lessons 1–3 establish this foundation.

**Our work and society:** examine opportunity, burdens and shared decisions. Lessons 4–5 broaden the view.

**My contribution and next step:** practise skills, take responsibility and keep learning. Lessons 6–8 bring the ideas into action.

These perspectives overlap. Revisit the earlier questions when a new situation is difficult. Completing the route means attempting and explaining choices, not agreeing with every example answer.
