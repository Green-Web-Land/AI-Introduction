Related original lessons: [clear instructions](https://github.com/Green-Web-Land/AI-Introduction/blob/6c4f0ae874b62ae99d1cf1dde9ee3dbb9b8aca83/courses/adults/lessons/03-clear-instructions.md), [checking results](https://github.com/Green-Web-Land/AI-Introduction/blob/6c4f0ae874b62ae99d1cf1dde9ee3dbb9b8aca83/courses/adults/lessons/04-check-results.md), [learning and growth](https://github.com/Green-Web-Land/AI-Introduction/blob/6c4f0ae874b62ae99d1cf1dde9ee3dbb9b8aca83/courses/adults/lessons/07-learning-growth.md), and [future scenarios](https://github.com/Green-Web-Land/AI-Introduction/blob/6c4f0ae874b62ae99d1cf1dde9ee3dbb9b8aca83/courses/adults/lessons/08-prediction.md).

“Your choices matter. Learn. Question. Decide.” is the central message and teaching sequence. The course connects individual learning with decisions about work and shared services.

All stories, notices, work maps, timings, answer keys and possible organisational choices are invented teaching material, drafted with AI assistance and checked during authoring. They are not measured AI outputs or results from a real workplace.

## Teaching choices and limits

The Learn–Question–Decide sequence, practice cycle, 30-day plan, final case and completion-time estimates are editorial choices. Learner effectiveness and reading-level suitability have not yet been tested. The facilitator guide proposes a review method.

No income, employment, productivity or learning gain is promised. The selected ILO sources do not establish those outcomes for an individual. Linked third-party content retains its own terms.

## Assets and versions

The visual preview uses original HTML/CSS composition and live-text sequences made for this course; no third-party photograph, icon library or remote font is included. Teaching text remains in Markdown. Edition: full adult course 0.2.1, published 6 October 2026.

## Keeping the material current

When revising an edition, recheck dated claims against the identified official sources and distinguish a changed source from a changed interpretation. Review examples when a reader reports a misleading assumption or unclear answer. Keep the edition, source record, Markdown and generated reading/print copies together. Do not describe a revised edition as learner-tested without evidence from that edition.
