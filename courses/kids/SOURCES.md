## Adult background

[UNICEF: Guidance on AI and children, version 3.0](https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/reports/policy-guidance-ai-children) was consulted on 2026-09-11. It supports child-centred priorities including safety, privacy, fairness, explanation, inclusion and development. It does not endorse this project, validate the chosen age range or establish that these lessons improve outcomes.

The lessons use basic explanatory writing and checkable fictional facts. Their reading level, timing and effectiveness are untested. No claim that children benefit more than adults has been established here. Future scenarios are authored possibilities, not evidence-based timelines or guarantees. No third-party images or child records are included.
