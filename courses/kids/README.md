# AI Introduction for Kids

A place to practise asking, thinking, checking and creating.

**First edition. Intended audience: ages 9–12, with a parent, teacher or another trusted adult.** The age range is a design target, not a tested reading-level result.

Start with the [curriculum](CURRICULUM.md), then try [What is AI?](lessons/01-what-is-ai.md) and [Be an answer detective](lessons/04-answer-detective.md). Adults should read the [parent and teacher guide](PARENT-TEACHER-GUIDE.md) first.

You need paper and a pencil, or you can talk through the activities. No AI account, installation, payment or online submission is needed. All characters, examples and pretend AI answers are invented for these lessons.

Our purpose is to help children grow in knowledge, confidence and judgment. Children are not responsible for improving or training AI. You may disagree with an answer, stop an activity or ask for help.

All nine lessons are ready in the curriculum, including Imagine your future and Fair rules for AI. Use the [glossary](GLOSSARY.md) for unfamiliar words. This is AI-assisted educational writing, reviewed by its author; it has not been tested with children. See [sources](SOURCES.md), the [reuse licence](LICENSE.md) and [developer guide](DEVELOPER-GUIDE.md).

The private review repository is AI-Introduction-Kids-review. AI-Introduction-Kids is the separate public destination; lesson publication follows owner review and the designated education legal decision.
