# 03 — Ask clearly

[Home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md) · [Word help](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Your mission:** Give a task, facts and limits.

Use paper, drawing or spoken answers with a trusted adult. No AI account is needed.

## The picnic problem

“Plan something fun” leaves many choices open. A helper cannot reliably know what you meant.

Here is a clearer request for a fictional indoor picnic:

> Suggest two quiet games for four people. We have paper and crayons. Each game must take ten minutes or less. No screens or running. Explain each game in three short steps. Ask if you need an important missing fact.

This request gives a **task**, **facts**, **limits** and an **answer format**. It uses invented details, not a real school name or address.

A pretend helper suggests a paper drawing game and a running race. The request was clear, but the race still breaks a rule. Clear instructions help describe what you want; they do not guarantee obedience or accuracy.

## Your turn

Rewrite “Help with my story” using this fact card:

- The main character is a fictional turtle.
- The story happens in an imaginary garden.
- You want three possible titles, not a whole story.
- Each title must have five words or fewer.
- Nothing frightening.

Keep your own ideas. Do not add personal details.

## Check together

One possible request:

> Suggest three gentle titles for my story about a turtle in an imaginary garden. Use five words or fewer per title. Do not write the story.

Try this pretend answer: “The Turtle's Garden”, “A Very Long and Terrifying Night in the Garden”, “A Small Green Home”.

The second title breaks both the length and tone limits. Count the words and explain the problem. You can ask for that title to be changed, or write your own.

**Finish:** describe one missing fact you would ask about before planning a real activity. For example, an adult may need to check accessibility needs. Do not invent an answer just to finish quickly.

All characters, situations and pretend AI responses in this lesson are invented teaching examples.
