# 02 — Ideas you can use

[Home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md) · [Word help](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Your mission:** Choose a small task while keeping your own choices.

Use paper, drawing or spoken answers with a trusted adult. No AI account is needed.

## Two endings

In this invented story, Jo finds a paper dragon in a library. Jo wants two possible endings, each kind and no more than two sentences long.

A pretend AI helper suggests:

A. The dragon helps Jo find a missing book. Jo makes it a paper bookmark as a thank-you.

B. The dragon steals everyone's books and locks the door. Nobody can read again.

Jo checks the request. A fits the kindness rule. B does not. Jo can also invent an ending without either suggestion: the dragon becomes a tiny reading lamp.

AI can suggest ideas, organise supplied information or help explain a word. A suggestion is a starting point. You decide whether it fits. Different people may like different stories; taste is different from a checkable fact.

## Your turn

Choose one small task:

- Invent two names for a fictional garden.
- Sort a supplied list of pretend art materials.
- Suggest a hint for a puzzle you already tried.

Write what you want to do yourself and what help you might ask for. Then decide whether these tasks are suitable:

1. Suggest a name for an imaginary spaceship.
2. Decide whether an unknown berry is safe to eat.
3. Write your whole school assignment and pretend you did it alone.

## Check together

1. A bounded creative task. You choose the name.
2. Stop and ask a trusted adult. Do not eat it based on an AI answer.
3. Follow the teacher's rules and do your own learning. A permitted hint or explanation may help; hiding who did the work does not.

**Finish:** invent your own kind dragon ending. Explain one choice you made. You do not need AI to be creative.

All characters, situations and pretend AI responses in this lesson are invented teaching examples.
