# 06 — Never Do with AI

[Home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md) · [Word help](../GLOSSARY.md)

**Your mission:** Replace unsafe choices with safer ones.

Use paper, drawing or spoken answers with a trusted adult. No AI account is needed.

## A pause is a useful choice

You do not have to continue when a tool asks for something private, hurtful or confusing. Stop and ask a trusted adult. If you already made a mistake, you still deserve help.

For these activities, use made-up information. Never enter passwords, home addresses, school details, private conversations, or personal pictures and voices into an AI tool. Do not share someone else's information as part of a game or exercise.

Never use AI to impersonate a real person, bully someone or make fake evidence. A picture can look real without showing a real event.

Never follow unchecked AI advice about danger or let a tool take actions you do not understand. Friendly wording is not a reason to trust it with secrets or let it replace people who care for you.

## Your turn: choose a safer path

All situations are invented.

1. A helper asks for your password to “improve your story.”
2. A friend wants a fake picture showing another child breaking a rule.
3. A chatbot gives a strange instruction about mixing household products.
4. A tool asks for a real school photo to make a classroom poster.

For each, name what you would stop and what you could do instead.

## Check together

1. Do not supply the password. Ask a trusted adult for help; a story exercise can use fictional details.
2. Do not create or spread false evidence. Make a clearly fictional cartoon with invented characters instead.
3. Do not try it. Move away from the activity and ask an adult. AI is not a safety check.
4. Use invented shapes or characters for this activity. Do not upload the photo.

## If something already happened

Tell a trusted adult what happened. You do not need to repeat upsetting content or send it to us. An adult can help secure an account or use an appropriate reporting route. If that adult is unavailable or dismisses the problem, try another trusted adult.

**Finish:** practise saying, “I don't understand what this will do. I'm stopping and asking for help.” Protecting yourself is more important than finishing a prompt.

All characters, situations and pretend AI responses in this lesson are invented teaching examples.
