# Curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

## Audience and preparation

High-school learners, approximately 13–18, with educator/supporter guidance. Bring paper or a local text editor and basic familiarity with arithmetic. No live AI, student account or real assignment is required. Equations and pseudocode are fully supplied; supporters can read them aloud or explain notation. The age guide is editorial and does not establish service eligibility.

## Method

Each lesson contains an objective, explanation, worked example, practice, reasoned answers and a transfer task. Attempt first, compare reasoning, correct the mistake and revisit an unfamiliar example. Use the fictional packet exactly as labelled; the original and corrected counts are different versions, not conflicting answer keys.

Suggested lesson time is 20–35 minutes, adjustable for reading, discussion and support. This estimate has not been trialled. Spread lessons over several sessions as useful; no deadline or public submission is required.

## Completion and reflection

The six-part portfolio in Lesson 10 samples permission, mathematical repair, evidence limits, code tracing, privacy and disclosure. Use [feedback criteria](EDUCATOR-GUIDE.md) to identify what to revisit. Completion is participation in an educational course, not a qualification, grade prediction or proof that every AI-related decision will be correct.
