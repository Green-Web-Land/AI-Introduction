## Authored teaching material

The assignment cards, library pilot, correction, assistant replies, equations, code traces, portfolio and future scenarios were created for this course with AI drafting assistance and editorial checking. They are not school documents, studies, student records, professional advice or real product transcripts. No external source is invented to make the fictional pilot appear real.

Lessons 2 and 9 deliberately use no jurisdiction-wide school-policy assertion. Ask the actual teacher about the actual assignment. Lesson 10's three/five-year stories have no assigned probability and make no grade, employment or social-outcome guarantee. Course suitability and learning impact have not been tested in a classroom.
