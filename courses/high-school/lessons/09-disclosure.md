# 9. Explain assistance honestly

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Describe what help was used without overstating your independent work.

## Explanation

Follow the actual assignment's permission and acknowledgement requirements. A truthful note should distinguish suggestions from decisions and identify relevant sources as required. There is no single disclosure sentence that satisfies every school.

Disclosure does not repair prohibited assistance. If you used help outside the rules, ask the teacher how to address it; do not erase the history or invent a different account. Do not treat an AI-use detector score as this course's proof of misconduct. This course uses the stated scenario and work record, not detection software.

## Worked example

**Fictional permitted-assistance record:** The practice task allowed hints and required a note. A learner requested a first-step hint, used the suggestion to divide both sides by 3, completed the equation and checked by substitution. No paragraph was generated.

Possible note: “For this practice task, I used a first-step AI hint. I completed the remaining calculation and checked it by substitution.” In this paper exercise the assistant reply is fictional; do not claim you actually used a service.

## Practice

Compare three statements about that record:

A. “No help was used.”
B. “A hint suggested the first operation; I completed and checked the calculation.”
C. “AI independently verified every answer in my assignment.”

Which is supported? Rewrite it to describe your actual offline course activity, including the fictional example.

## Answer and reasoning

Try the practice before reading this section.

B matches the fictional record. A hides help; C adds a verification claim that the record does not support. For the real activity here: “I worked through a supplied fictional AI-hint example on paper and checked the calculation.” If you received other help, describe it accurately rather than copying this sentence unchanged.

## Transfer task

New fictional record: a permitted critique identified an unsupported learning claim in a library paragraph; the learner revised it using Record A. Write a note distinguishing critique, revision and source. Example: “A critique prompted me to check the claim. I revised the paragraph using the fictional pilot record.” Add any tool, date or citation details only if true and required by the actual task.
