# Developer and adaptation guide

[Course home](README.md)

## Package

Ten Markdown lessons, this course home, curriculum, glossary, educator guide, source map, asset provenance and licence; five SVG originals and five PNG renderings. No executable product, API integration, quiz backend, tracking script or learner-data form is included. Hosting through GitHub has its own platform practices; static content does not establish that a host collects nothing.

Preserve the course-relative links and images directory when copying. PNGs are readable GitHub renderings; SVGs are editable originals. Each diagram has descriptive alt text and a text equivalent in [asset notes](ASSET-SOURCES.md). Keep meaning outside colour alone. Test reading order, contrast, zoom and assistive-technology behaviour in your own integration; no formal accessibility compliance is claimed here.

## Learning behaviour to preserve

Keep objectives, worked examples, practice, answer reasoning and transfer tasks. Answers are intentionally available for self-checking; an app may offer a reveal control but must not require an account or payment merely to replicate this course's offline activities. That recommendation describes our learning design, not an additional licence restriction.

Keep policy cards and source packets visibly fictional. Do not turn them into claims about actual schools or research. Preserve original/corrected count labels. Do not present a learner's portfolio as professional certification. Keep future scenarios conditional and age guidance editorial.

## Integration boundaries

Live AI, analytics, account systems, storage or submitted work are separate features requiring their own technical, school-policy and legal assessment. A licence to reuse text does not settle those obligations or give rights to third-party personal data. Do not silently add prompts that send student work to a service.

The [licence](LICENSE.md) permits commercial adaptation subject to its terms. Credit the source, link the licence and mark changes. Third-party reference pages are not bundled under our licence. Recheck time-sensitive references and rules when releasing an adaptation. This package is education material, not an endorsed tool or product benchmark.
