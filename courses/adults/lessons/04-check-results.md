# Check and improve results

[Home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

**Your goal:** find a specific error, correct it and check the revised result.

A polished answer can still fail the task. Check it against the original request and appropriate evidence, not just against how confident it sounds. Asking the same system whether it is correct can help uncover issues, but is not independent verification.

## Use four checks

1. **Requirements:** did the answer include what you asked for and respect the limits?
2. **Facts:** do names, quantities, dates and claims match the source?
3. **Reasoning and calculations:** do the steps follow, and can important calculations be reproduced with an appropriate tool?
4. **Consequences:** is the answer ready for its intended use, and who needs to review it?

When a source is cited, open the actual source. Confirm it exists, addresses the same question and supports the particular claim. Consider its date and authority. Two pages repeating the same unsupported statement are not two independent confirmations.

## Worked example: a supplies summary

Fictional facts: notebooks cost 4 units each and you need 3; pens cost 2 units each and you need 5. Delivery cost is unknown. Request: “Calculate item costs and list unknown costs separately.”

Deliberately flawed authored answer: “Three notebooks cost 12; five pens cost 8. Total including free delivery: 20. Order today for a discount.”

There are several faults: pens cost 10, the item subtotal is 22, delivery is unknown, and no discount was supplied. A corrected answer is: “Notebooks: 12. Pens: 10. Item subtotal: 22. Delivery: unknown. Final total cannot yet be determined.”

The check is simple multiplication and addition; no AI is necessary to verify it. Do not treat a corrected subtotal as a final invoice.

## Give precise feedback

Instead of “try harder,” say: “Five pens at 2 each cost 10. Delivery was not supplied. Recalculate the item subtotal, list delivery as unknown and remove the discount claim.” Then check the full revised answer: correcting one error does not guarantee other details stayed correct.

## Exercise

Facts: a fictional session lasts 45 minutes, admits 8 participants and requires a notebook. No recording arrangement is specified. You requested a two-sentence description.

Flawed answer: “Join our 60-minute session for up to 8 participants. A recording will be available.”

Identify the inaccurate detail, omitted requirement and unsupported claim. Write a correction request and a corrected description.

**Answer guidance:** replace 60 with 45, include the notebook and remove the recording promise or flag it as unknown. One valid description is: “Join our 45-minute session for up to 8 participants. Bring a notebook.” Verify that it has two sentences and preserves all supplied facts.

You pass when your correction names the actual mismatch and your final check returns to the original facts. If you cannot verify an important claim, mark it unresolved and seek suitable evidence before using it.
