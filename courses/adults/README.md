# AI Introduction

Learn to use AI with clear goals, useful instructions and your own judgment.

**Draft for private review.** The first edition contains nine drafted lessons with exercises and answer guidance. It is ready for content review, not legally cleared for public release.

This course is for adult beginners. You can read the explanations and complete the paper exercises without installing software, creating an account or paying for an AI service. Examples are fictional and do not require personal information.

Start with the [curriculum](CURRICULUM.md), or try the [sample lesson: Give clear instructions](lessons/03-clear-instructions.md). See [sources and content notes](SOURCES.md).

Explore [Prediction: Your life with AI in X years](lessons/08-prediction.md) for possible futures, the assumptions behind them and a worksheet for choosing your own next step. The scenarios are possibilities, not guaranteed forecasts.

The goal is to strengthen your ability to choose a task, explain what you need, check the result and decide what to do next. You remain responsible for the decisions and actions you take.

Our foundation is: **AI should expand human capability while preserving human choice, responsibility and the ability to question it.** Read [Universal Principles for AI](lessons/09-universal-principles.md) for our proposed principles, practical examples and the distinction between values, enforceable laws, product policies and technical safeguards. These are not presented as an existing worldwide rulebook.

## Relationship to WebAI

AI Introduction is an independently usable educational project intended for possible integration into WebAI. Reading the course does not require WebAI. These lessons do not claim that a particular application supports every example.

Developers should inspect private drafts as drafts. Our own public integration requires an approved content revision; do not automatically pull changing drafts into our released products. See the developer integration guide below for version and reuse boundaries.
