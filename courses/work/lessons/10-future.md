# 10. Build a repeatable workflow

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Evaluate useful assistance without promising employment outcomes.

## Explanation

Our suggested sequence is: define the task, check permission, choose suitable inputs, draft, verify, review any action, and record the result. You may use ordinary tools or no AI. This teaching method has not been validated as a productivity intervention.

Compare the quality of the checked outcome and the total effort, including correction time. A fast draft that needs extensive repair is not automatically a saving. Use invented measurements for this exercise rather than monitoring real employees.

## Worked example

Fictional timing example: manual drafting and checking take 30 minutes. Assisted drafting takes 10, review takes 12 and correction takes 10: total 32. In this one example assistance takes 2 minutes longer. It does not prove all AI use is slower.

## Prediction: possible work in three and five years

In three years, a worker who regularly practises verification might build stronger habits of explaining decisions. Overreliance on unexamined output might conceal gaps. Neither outcome is inevitable.

In five years, appropriate training, access and safeguards could support useful changes to work. Poor implementation or unequal access could bring different results. These are conditional scenarios, not forecasts with probabilities or promises of jobs, income, promotion or reduced workload.

## Practice

Build a paper portfolio: bounded brief; permission decision; fictional input choice; corrected summary; checked workload calculation; proposed action boundary; and handoff. Include one thing that remains uncertain.

For timing transfer, use invented values: assisted draft 8 minutes, review 9, correction 3, versus manual total 30. Calculate the difference but state what the comparison cannot establish.

## Answer and reasoning

Try the practice before reading this section.

The assisted total is 20 minutes, 10 fewer than the manual example. That does not establish equivalent quality or a general saving. Compare the actual output and context as well. Use the [facilitator guide](../FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md) for portfolio feedback; completing it is not a qualification.

## Transfer task

Write a three-year possibility as “If …, then … might …; this depends on …; I would check ….” Name a skill you want to keep without AI. A strong response states assumptions and evidence that could change the plan. No personal career details or public sharing are required.
