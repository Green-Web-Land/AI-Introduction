# Developer and adaptation guide

[Course home](README.md)

Ten Markdown lessons, eight supporting documents and five SVG/PNG pairs. No application code, API integration, tracking script, account or submission form is included. Host practices are separate from these files.

Keep relative links, lesson identifiers and images intact. SVGs are editable originals; PNGs are display versions. Retain alt text and [text equivalents](ASSET-SOURCES.md). Check reading order, contrast, zoom and assistive-technology behaviour in your own interface; no formal accessibility conformance is claimed.

Preserve distinctions between facts, assumptions, drafts, actions, sending and acknowledgement. Keep all fictional labels and corrected-version notes. Do not turn the course's review examples into a claim that your app implements permission enforcement. Prompts alone do not implement access controls.

An optional reveal-answer interface can support practice, but retain the offline path. This is design guidance, not an additional licence restriction. Do not repurpose the portfolio as an employee ranking or hiring assessment.

Pin a chosen content revision when copying it. [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE.md) permits commercial adaptation subject to its terms and rights held: give credit, retain notices, link the licence and identify changes. External references are not bundled under the course licence.

New mailbox actions, live AI, analytics, stored work or operational integrations require their own technical, privacy and organizational review. Content reuse permission is not authority to process another party's data or act in workplace systems.
