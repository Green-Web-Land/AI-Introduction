## Authored material

The service notes, policy cards, prompts, replies, workload and timing data, handoffs, review slips and future scenarios were authored for this course with AI assistance and editorial checking. They are not real company records, actual employee information, measured product outputs or external research instruments.

The embedded instruction example is a harmless paper illustration, not an attack test or a claim of technical protection. The arithmetic is limited to supplied numbers. Scenarios in three/five years have no assigned probabilities or guaranteed economic outcomes.

No workplace trial, professional advice, formal accessibility certification or complete organizational compliance assessment is claimed. For real decisions, use the actual workplace's rules and appropriate responsible contacts. Recheck changing references when adapting the material.
