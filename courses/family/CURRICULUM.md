# Curriculum

Audience: adult beginners. No prerequisite course, account or personal disclosure. Read at your own pace. Supplied exercises can be completed offline and skipped without explanation.

## Completion exercise

Fictional facts: Dana can consider a 15-minute chat between 16:00 and 16:30 without recording. Eli is available 16:10–16:40. Neither has accepted an invitation.

Sample draft: Our recorded 16:00–16:30 call is booked. Your silence proves you agree, and I will publish the story.

Identify the timing, agreement, privacy and action errors. Write a fitting proposal without assuming acceptance.

Answer guidance: overlap is 16:10–16:30, 20 minutes. The sample starts before Eli is available and runs 30 rather than 15 minutes. Recording conflicts with the limit; booking, consent from silence and publication are unsupported. One proposal is: Would 16:10–16:25 work for a 15-minute chat without recording? Please let me know if you would like to join; it is fine to decline. Nothing is booked or sent by this exercise.

## Self-review

Explain the observation/interpretation distinction, preserve a boundary in a rewrite, identify separate permission needs, check a time interval and acknowledge a decline. State when a course exercise should stop. Revisit lessons as needed. This is not a validated assessment, a relationship score or a professional qualification.
