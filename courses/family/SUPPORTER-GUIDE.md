# Supporter guide

[Course home](README.md)

Ask what assistance is wanted: reading aloud, dictation, term explanation or comparing a fictional answer. Do not ask learners to prove relevance by sharing family history. Keep the learner's choice to skip, pause or stop.

## A session

1. Read the objective and fictional facts.
2. Allow an attempt before showing answers.
3. Ask which statement is supported and which remains unknown.
4. Compare the answer with the guidance; accept alternatives that preserve constraints.
5. Offer the transfer task without taking over.

Read table labels aloud and use image text equivalents if useful. No colour distinction is needed. Pace and format are choices; the course does not prescribe session length.

## Boundaries for supporters

Do not mediate a real dispute, diagnose someone, inspect messages or ask learners to contact anyone. A corrected draft is not proof that contact is welcome or safe. Respect no-contact boundaries and declines. Do not use the lesson to press either participant to reconcile or accept blame.

If a person mentions a real safety concern, stop the exercise and support access to appropriate help when safe. Do not investigate the details for a course answer. General support information is in [lesson 9](lessons/09-limits.md).

No learner trial, clinical review or professional accessibility assessment has been conducted. Completion does not establish relationship health or decision-making capacity.
