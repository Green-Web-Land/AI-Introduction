# How to Use AI for Family Connection and Relationships

[AI Introduction home](../../README.md)

**Course introduction · full course planned.**

Using assistance to support communication while respecting people’s choices, privacy and boundaries.

## Planned coverage

Rehearse respectful wording; distinguish guesses from facts; seek consent; preserve direct human conversation.

## Example of a planned activity

Rewrite a fictional invitation in your own voice without uploading private messages.

This is an outline of a future activity, not a completed lesson or a tested learning outcome.

## Audience and boundaries

Activities will not diagnose relationships, encourage monitoring or manipulation, require disclosures, or pressure reconciliation in unsafe situations.

Core activities will use fictional information and offer an offline route. No WebAI installation or AI account will be required. Full lessons will state their audience, practical requirements, sources and guidance for supporters.
