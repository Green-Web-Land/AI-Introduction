# How to Use AI for Family Connection and Relationships

[AI Introduction home](../../README.md)

Learn to use AI well—and remain able to think, choose and act for yourself.

A complete course for adult beginners who want to practise communication while respecting privacy, consent and boundaries. Family can mean relatives, partners, chosen family or people living apart. There is no required family structure or level of contact.

## Start here

All activities use fictional information. No AI account, installation, payment, private disclosure or real message is required. Work on paper, type or ask a supporter to read aloud. Try the exercises before the answer guidance. Choose your pace, skip a scenario or stop without explaining why.

This adult-focused material is not designed as an unsupervised course for children. It is general education, not therapy, mediation, legal advice or a safety plan. It does not diagnose relationships or promise reconciliation. Contact is never a required course outcome.

## What you can practise

Separate facts from guesses; draft a clear request; preserve your limits; distinguish permission for different uses; check a shared plan; respect a decline; recognize when drafting is inappropriate. These are learning objectives, not measured relationship outcomes.

## Choice and support

Use original fictional examples rather than private messages or pictures. AI output is not evidence of another person's intentions. For real threats or fear, pause this course and seek appropriate help when safe; [lesson 9](lessons/09-limits.md) provides a general support reference.

AI-assisted writing and diagrams were editorially checked. No learner trial, clinical review or formal accessibility evaluation has been conducted. Future scenarios are conditional possibilities, not guarantees.
