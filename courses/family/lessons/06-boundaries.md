# 6. Keep your boundary when revising

[Previous](05-privacy.md) · [Course home](../README.md) · [Next](07-plans.md)

## Objective

Write a clear limit without invented accusations.

## Explanation

Respectful wording does not require agreeing, accepting blame or giving up your limits. Describe what you are willing to do, specifically and truthfully. Avoid personality labels or claims to know motives.

A softer tone cannot guarantee a response or make an unsafe interaction safe. These are ordinary fictional planning disagreements, not threat or abuse exercises. For those concerns, skip drafting and use lesson 9.

## Worked example

Kim can help for 30 minutes Saturday, no longer.

Pressuring draft: You always exploit me and must stop being selfish.

Bounded draft: I can help for 30 minutes Saturday. I cannot stay longer. Please plan without additional help from me after that.

The limit remains without an invented history or diagnosis.

## Practice

Rowan is unavailable for calls after 20:00 and does not want to promise another time yet.

Sample: You never respect anyone. I will answer whenever you need me.

Find the unsupported accusation and contradiction. Rewrite while preserving the limit.

## Answers and checking

“Never respect anyone” is unsupported. “Whenever” contradicts availability. Example: I am not available for calls after 20:00. I am not ready to suggest another time. No apology, explanation or additional promise is required.

## Try independently

Write a fictional task boundary and inspect a rewrite for expanded commitments. Reject wording that changes your intended limit.
