# 10. Choose a routine and examine possibilities

[Previous](09-limits.md) · [Course home](../README.md)

## Objective

Separate conditional scenarios from relationship promises.

## Explanation

A routine can support your own editing without imposing participation on another person. Check what you control: facts, wording and boundaries. Agreement is not a score of your worth or a guaranteed outcome of prompting.

The futures below are possibilities, not forecasts with probabilities. Costs, preferences, reliability and circumstances may change. Human interaction, ordinary notes or less contact may be preferable.

## Worked example

Personal drafting routine: clarify 5 minutes, write 10, check 5: total 20. Nothing must be sent; you can stop.

Three-year possibility: if drafting help is useful, a person might collect wording that sounds like them. Five-year possibility: improved, affordable tools might simplify some planning. Counter-scenario: checking burdens or a dislike of mediated communication might favour ordinary notes. None guarantees closeness, reconciliation or wellbeing.

## Practice

A. Make a 15-minute routine including checking and an option not to send.

B. Rewrite: AI will fix every family within five years.

C. Give two reasons to change or stop a routine.

D. State a condition, possible benefit, uncertainty and non-AI alternative.

## Answers and checking

A: clarify 3, draft 7, check 5: total 15; not sending remains valid. B: reject the promise. Under suitable conditions a tool might help edit a draft; other people's choices and outcomes remain uncertain. C: invented facts, pressure, changed preferences or a simpler method. D: continued usefulness; clearer wording; uncertain fit; handwritten notes.

## Try independently

Revise a supplied fictional draft: preserve facts, remove assumptions, protect privacy, keep both people’s choices and state whether action is authorized. No qualification or measured relationship benefit is implied.
