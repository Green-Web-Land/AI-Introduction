# 8. Respect a decline

[Previous](07-plans.md) · [Course home](../README.md) · [Next](09-limits.md)

## Objective

Respond without surveillance, guilt or invented motives.

## Explanation

A decline concerns an invitation; it is not a diagnosis. Acknowledge it without investigating private accounts, tracking activity or generating pressure. More persuasive wording is not necessarily better.

Separate what you can decide from what belongs to someone else. Acknowledgment does not require accepting an inaccurate accusation. You may also choose not to continue a conversation. These choices do not override safety concerns or legal restrictions.

## Worked example

Reply: I will not join the booklet project.

Bad draft: AI says you fear commitment, so I will ask daily.

Alternative: Thanks for letting me know. I will plan without your contribution.

No motive is invented and no repeated contact promised.

## Practice

Choose a response:

A. Ask AI to infer a diagnosis.

B. Search private messages for a reason.

C. Accept the answer and adjust the project.

D. Draft guilt until they change their mind.

What fact can you retain for planning?

## Answers and checking

C respects the decision. A invents a diagnosis, B invades privacy and D applies pressure. The supplied fact is non-participation in this project. Reasons and feelings remain unknown. Do not generalize it into “they never care.”

## Try independently

Write an acknowledgment in your style. Remove any demand, invented motive or hidden request to reconsider.
