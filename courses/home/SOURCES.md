The task-selection method, constraints, schedules, invented costs, creative writing, product card, action examples and future scenarios were authored with AI assistance and editorial checking. They are not medical guidance, actual prices, product instructions, real household data or research findings.

No practical health/safety procedure is taught. Lesson 8 teaches checking relevant evidence and seeking qualified help rather than acting on an unfamiliar hazardous suggestion. The fictional product card is used only to compare a claim with stated limits. Three/five-year scenarios have no assigned probabilities or guarantees.

The course has no learner trial, formal accessibility evaluation or demonstrated effect on savings or wellbeing. Recheck changing references and actual requirements when adapting.
