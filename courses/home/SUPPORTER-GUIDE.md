# Supporter guide

[Course home](README.md)

## Prepare and adapt

Read the lessons and answers first. Intended for adult beginners; suitability has not been tested with learners. Offer paper, typed or spoken responses and breaks. No personal story or household record is needed. Do not judge someone's ability, finances or home from a response.

All required work is offline. A learner may choose no AI or decline an activity. Use invented details rather than asking for real addresses, health information or private conversations. Respect other people's choices about sharing.

## Exercise distinctions

The original activity plan totals 60 minutes. Its shorter version totals 40, preserving a 10-minute break and 5 minutes for putting away. These are fictional constraints, not a health recommendation. An 8-minute interruption leaves 32 minutes; after the fixed 20 minutes, 12 remain for activities.

The materials card is separate: original total 16 units; four cards give 18; a pencil price of 5 with original quantities gives 17. Cost units and charges are invented, not market information. No purchase follows.

The display-stand card is fictional. A match to its stated limits proves only consistency with the exercise, not safety of any real product. No practical repair or hazardous procedure is included.

## Portfolio feedback

| Part | Look for |
|---|---|
| Brief | Goal, output and clear limits |
| Inputs | Invented details and respect for privacy |
| Plan | Correct total and available materials |
| Schedule | Stated breaks, transitions and explicit tradeoffs |
| Calculation | Correct quantities, price version and totals |
| Creativity | A choice or revision the learner can explain |
| Claim | Appropriate evidence and remaining uncertainty |
| Action | Preparation separated from purchase, booking, sending or device control |

Use descriptive feedback about the work, not a score about the person. Ask which constraint or source supports an answer. This is not a validated test, medical assessment or professional credential.

If a real specialised concern arises, leave the exercise and seek an appropriate qualified source. Do not request details publicly or try an unfamiliar procedure to test AI. Future scenarios are conditional; discuss access and choice without promising outcomes.

Preserve fictional labels, answers, [sources](SOURCES.md) and [licence notices](LICENSE.md). Accessibility of any adapted delivery needs its own checking.
