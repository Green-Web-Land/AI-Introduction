# Curriculum

[Course home](README.md)

Adult beginners; no prerequisite course, AI account or installation. Paper and basic arithmetic are enough for the exercises. A local editor, calculator or spoken response can be used if helpful.

| Lesson | Intended outcome |
|---|---|
| [1. Choose help that fits](lessons/01-choice.md) | Choose a small task and a way to check the result. |
| [2. Keep household information private](lessons/02-privacy.md) | Use invented examples and respect another person's choice. |
| [3. Describe useful constraints](lessons/03-brief.md) | Give enough context without supplying private details. |
| [4. Check an activity plan](lessons/04-plan.md) | Compare a suggestion with every supplied constraint. |
| [5. Revise time without hiding tradeoffs](lessons/05-schedule.md) | Build a shorter plan while keeping stated needs visible. |
| [6. Check a list and its arithmetic](lessons/06-materials.md) | Use supplied quantities and fictional prices correctly. |
| [7. Keep your own creative choices](lessons/07-create.md) | Use a suggestion as a starting point you can revise. |
| [8. Check practical claims before acting](lessons/08-claims.md) | Match a claim to the right evidence and recognise limits. |
| [9. Keep actions under your control](lessons/09-actions.md) | Separate a suggestion from permission to buy, book or control devices. |
| [10. Build a useful routine](lessons/10-future.md) | Evaluate assistance while retaining choice and independent skills. |

Suggested 20–30-minute lessons are flexible and untested. Keep the 60-minute and 40-minute activity plans distinct. The cost card is a separate hypothetical project, not a requirement to purchase materials for the activity.

The final portfolio includes a brief, input choice, checked plan, revised schedule, calculation, creative change, claim check and action boundary. Use [supporter feedback](SUPPORTER-GUIDE.md) to find what needs revision. Completion is participation, not a qualification or measure of household competence.
