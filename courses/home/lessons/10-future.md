# 10. Build a useful routine

[Course home](../README.md) · [Curriculum](../CURRICULUM.md)

## Objective

Evaluate assistance while retaining choice and independent skills.

## Explanation

Try a small process: choose a task, limit inputs, examine the suggestion, revise it, and decide whether it helped. AI may be useful, unnecessary or unsuitable for a particular task. You do not need to automate a home to complete this course.

Judge the full effort, including checking and correction. A pleasant-looking plan is not proof of savings or improved wellbeing.

## Worked example

Fictional timing: planning manually takes 12 minutes. Assisted drafting takes 4, checking 5 and revision 5: total 14. That is 2 minutes longer in this example, not evidence that all AI use is slower.

## Prediction: possibilities in three and five years

In three years, practising checks might help you keep a collection of plans you understand and can adapt. Following suggestions without checking might hide mistakes. Neither result is inevitable.

In five years, accessible tools and appropriate safeguards could offer more options for household planning. Poor design, unequal access or unwanted automation could limit benefits. These are conditional scenarios, not predictions with assigned probabilities or promises of savings, happiness or independence.

## Practice

Create a portfolio: bounded brief; invented input; checked 60-minute plan; shorter schedule; materials calculation; creative change; practical-claim check; action boundary. Use the [supporter guide](../SUPPORTER-GUIDE.md) to review your reasoning.

## Answer and reasoning

Attempt the practice before reading this section.

A complete response shows why each choice fits its stated constraints and what remains unknown. Correct numbers are only part of the check. You may decline an activity or use no AI. This portfolio is education, not a qualification or assessment of household competence.

## Transfer task

Write “If …, then … might …; this depends on …; I would check …” about your life in three years. Name one activity you want to remain able to do without AI. Keep it private if you prefer; no personal details or public submission are required.
